CASE FILE / 01

Supply Chain & Inventory Intelligence

Make replenishment risk visible before it hurts.

For a fictional multi-warehouse distributor, inventory data lived across operational tables with no shared view of SKU health, stockout exposure, or supplier reliability.

DATA SCALE
237,600 inventory records
MODEL
Sales, purchases, products, suppliers, warehouses, calendar
OUTPUT
Decision-ready Power BI operating dashboard

Questions framed

  • Which SKUs are most often approaching a stockout?
  • Where is cash tied up in slow-moving stock?
  • Which suppliers are adding replenishment risk?
  • What should management watch every week?

Approach

  1. Validate relational data and define KPI logic
  2. Use SQL to identify risk patterns and exceptions
  3. Model inventory, demand, and supplier performance
  4. Translate findings into replenishment priorities
DESIGNED TO ANSWER“What needs attention now?”
CASE FILE / 02

Workforce Operations & SLA Analytics

See the chain from absence to client outcome.

A synthetic workforce dataset models 10 client sites, 150 employees, 12 months of shifts, attendance, overtime, incidents, and service outcomes—one system, not six disconnected reports.

OPERATING SCOPE
10 sites / 150 people
TIME WINDOW
12 months of shift activity
KEY LINK
Staffing coverage → SLA performance

Questions framed

  • Where does absenteeism turn into overtime?
  • Which sites are consistently short of planned coverage?
  • How is labour cost moving against service levels?
  • Which operating patterns precede incidents?

Deliverables

  1. Site-level SLA and coverage scorecard
  2. Attendance and overtime variance view
  3. Monthly trend and exception analysis
  4. Action list for operations leadership
DESIGNED TO REVEAL“Where is the operation leaking performance?”
CASE FILE / 03

SaaS Client Onboarding Workflow Redesign

Turn handoffs into a visible delivery system.

A fictional B2B SaaS team was losing momentum between sales handoff, implementation setup, and go-live. The intervention was a clearer workflow—not another dashboard.

PROBLEM
Delayed launches and unclear ownership
ROLE
Business analyst / process designer
OUTCOME TARGET
Shorter, more predictable onboarding

BA toolkit

  • Stakeholder and pain-point map
  • As-is and to-be workflow design
  • Prioritised requirements and user stories
  • Wireframes and acceptance criteria

Example user story

As a customer success manager, I want to see onboarding status and blocked items for every account, so that I can intervene before the target go-live date.

Acceptance signal: accountable owner and outstanding item are visible in a single view.

DESIGNED TO CREATE“A launch process the team can actually steer.”
